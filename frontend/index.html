How are plans scored and checked?

Cost = $0.50 × passenger delay minutes + $250 × missed connecting passengers + $30 × flight delay minutes + $4,500 per reserve aircraft + $1,200 per reserve crew. These are illustrative weights, not airline prices. Passenger minutes count boarded flight segments; missed-connection cost is an additional penalty. There is no rebooking model.

Hard checks: aircraft position and 30m turns; crew position, 20m transfers, qualification, 30m report lead, 15m release, 690m duty, 6 segments, 420m flight time, 600m prior rest; seat capacity; 15m gate service windows; weather movement closures. Crew rules are synthetic and do not establish regulatory legality. All clocks use the same synthetic day, with no time zones.
