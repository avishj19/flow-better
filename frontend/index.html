Four-pillar formulas & modeling assumptions

Financial: $100 × operated flight-delay minutes + $10,000 × ferry flights + $15,000 × cancelled flights + $3,000 × reserve crews.

Passengers: Σ(booked passengers × delay minutes) + 250 × missed connecting passengers. Cancellation assumes 1,440 minutes of delay per booked passenger; no real rebooking is claimed.

Network: 20,000 points per aircraft not at its planned overnight hub by its cutoff. Lower is better. This is a soft next-day readiness penalty.

Crew buffer: minimum remaining minutes across operating crews = report + modeled duty allowance − actual final release. Negative buffer blocks approval, as do other hard constraints. Simplified Part 117-inspired checks include qualification, rest, segments and flight time; actual FAA legality is not evaluated.

ORD context: a bounded parser extracts “add 45 mins” from the preserved synthetic ground-ops message. For affected arrivals, aircraft turnaround becomes 30 + 45 = 75 minutes, which propagates into flight timing and all four scores. Gate service windows remain a separate simplification.

No combined weighted score and no claim that competitors lack explainability. Local rationale is generated from verified math; optional AI prose cannot override it.
